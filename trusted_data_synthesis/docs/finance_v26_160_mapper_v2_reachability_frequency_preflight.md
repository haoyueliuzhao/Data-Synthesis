## Interpretation

v26.160 establishes that one fresh, model-unexposed Mapper-v2 Reachability-frequency experiment
is statically and dynamically executable under a fully bound bounded generation condition. It
does not establish that the model will complete all 360 endpoints, that the Measurement Gate will
pass, that every strong cell will contain a Qualified row, or that any State has positive or
non-degenerate probability.

The successor may execute only the exact 360-Job Manifest under the exact v26.160 Runner,
resource, Support, Verifier, Tool, Mapper, condition, Assignment, estimand, and outcome Contracts.
Any Measurement Support, Instrument, Privacy, exact-model/Thinking/Usage, budget-no-call, or
unresolved Transport failure keeps every frequency estimand null. A complete run still requires
an independent postrun audit before any frequency result can be treated as evidence.
