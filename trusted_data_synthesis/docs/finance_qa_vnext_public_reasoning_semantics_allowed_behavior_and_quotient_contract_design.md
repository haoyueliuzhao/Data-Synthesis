## Fixed Task Semantics And Variable Public Behavior

The scientific object is public evidence-grounded decisions and their causal updates. Private
chain of thought is not requested, recorded or treated as the object. The Contract separates:

| Boundary | Meaning in this design |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed Task | Exact question, entity, periods, metric definitions and objective |
| Fixed verification | Evidence universe and authority/visibility rules, answer Oracle, units, Schema, tolerance, rounding, citations and required public obligations |
| Variable public behavior | Actual sufficient support selection, typed decision basis, derivation dependencies and Observation-grounded updates |
| Operational variation | Runtime identity, nonauthoritative wording, exact numeric surface form and lawful independent scheduling |

An unchanged allowed Evidence universe does not require every route to select an identical
support subset. Any different subset must satisfy the unchanged role, source, visibility and
citation constraints. An axis without an available admissible alternative is empty or not
witnessed; the design does not add Evidence or relax a role binding to populate it.

The answer Oracle remains the exact answer-correctness authority. It does not prescribe the
candidate's decision identities, operation route or realized causal graph. All required public
obligations still have to be discharged. The historical five-step execute-only DAG is a verified
local subdomain, not a universal route grammar. An alternative requires typed execution and an
Evidence-to-obligation discharge mapping; absent a suitable validator, it remains unimplemented.

In particular, F1/F2 require `derived_growth_absolute_spread`: the exact absolute difference
between revenue and operating-income growth rates. A route that only determines which rate is
larger does not satisfy that answer contract. Neither a changed answer Oracle nor an ungrounded
method label can be used to manufacture a second class.

## Equivalence And Conditional Semantic Difference

The future quotient domain contains independently Qualified own-execution trajectories for one
fixed Task and predeclared Contract. The intended relation is exact equality of a canonical
typed public causal representation, up to semantic-label-preserving graph isomorphism.
No general graph canonicalizer, trajectory projector or formal Mapper is implemented here.

Runtime IDs may be renamed only through a bijection preserving actual producers and every
reference. Independent Actions commute only if they have no conflicting Claim or State effects.
Wording is ignored only when typed propositions and decision bases are unchanged. Finite Decimal
surface values are normalized by exact equality; tolerance-based pairwise closeness is not an
equivalence relation. Ordered operand roles remain ordered unless a registered commutative rule
permits otherwise. Source aliases require preregistered content, locator, role and authority
preservation; equal numeric values alone cannot merge different evidential grounds.

The prospective representation retains actual support and its use edges, typed basis relations,
registered operation semantics and operand roles, producer-to-consumer Claim dependencies,
Claim values/units/grounding, and meaningful rejection or revision causality. Revision includes
its supporting Observation, old/replacement Claim semantics, downstream invalidation and State
effects. Tentative or invalidated Claims cannot become verified inputs. Terminal disposition and
answer/citation semantics remain retained; changing terminal policy is outside the current
change-request domain and cannot waive the frozen answer contract.

Equal final answers do not establish behavior equivalence. Different graph hashes, Program
hashes, route labels, padding or callback order do not establish behavioral difference. A future
separation needs two own-qualified executions and a replay-supported difference in retained
semantics under the predeclared rules. Unregistered algebraic rewrites are outside the current
design domain until registered. Invalid, unreplayed or cross-Task candidates do not form new
same-Task classes. No valid alternative route or second class is assumed to exist.

Qualification remains the noncompensatory conjunction `QA_valid AND trajectory_valid`.
QA validity requires source, answer and citation validity; trajectory validity requires
pre-action, grounding, reasoning/Action, Observation/Update and critical-coverage validity.
A correct answer cannot override an invalid public trajectory.

typed basis, strategy, operands and the next Action before execution. It may propose an update
after receiving the actual Observation and before the next State commit. Ownership applies only
to semantic fields present in that corresponding recorded, phase-correct proposal.

The Host derives legal candidates from frozen rules and the current State, resolves references,
executes the selected registered Action, persists commitments and receipts, validates updates,
applies State changes and recomputes the Oracle. It must not filter routes toward the known
answer or silently replace an unsupported choice with a successful route. Future provenance
must distinguish `frozen_contract`, `host_derived`, `model_proposed`, and `deterministic_fixture`
field origins and bind proposal, admitted Action, transformation and actual execution.

A Host-scripted trajectory is not model-owned. This stage observes no model proposals and
establishes no model reachability, reasoning contribution, state probability or training value.
These are explicit future responsibility requirements, not empirical findings from this stage.
