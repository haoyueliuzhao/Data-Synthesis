## Current Boundary

The authorization is now formally present and verified, but remains unconsumed. The next stage
may access credentials only after exact admission succeeds and may execute only the frozen 192
Jobs through the successor integration. It must preserve Raw-before-Result ordering and construct
fresh typed Outcome evidence. It may report actual Provider, terminal, validity, and persistence
results from that run, including a failed or interrupted run, but it may not silently replace,
reuse, rerun, recover, respecify, or expand the denominator.

QA remains an isolated offline side path and cannot enter this Manifest. Capability estimation,
Mapper, State, frequency, Contribution, VTDO, training, release, and production require later
evidence and separate authorization after the exact online execution is complete.
