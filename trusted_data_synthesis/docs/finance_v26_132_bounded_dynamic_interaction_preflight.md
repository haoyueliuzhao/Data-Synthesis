## Frozen Dynamic Class

The new ordinary-detour policy separates four counters:

```text
ABI Rescue             response serialization recovery
Semantic Recovery      rejected semantic choice recovery
Transport Replacement  no-payload transport replacement
Ordinary Detour        legal successful action with no immediate public semantic progress
```

The frozen allowance is one Ordinary Detour. An Ordinary Detour is counted only after the exact
visible Action has crossed the Action ABI, produced the same reversible Stage 2 Commit, completed
a successful public Tool Observation, and left the public progress vector unchanged. It consumes
none of the three recovery counters and does not erase prior failure or progress records.

The exact qualified class is deliberately narrower than all legal model trajectories:

```text
registered reference prefix
+ at most one successful no-progress public Action
+ ordinary deterministic replanning that still closes Program, verification, and Final
```

This is the bounded class `T_dyn^(1)` established by this preflight. It is not a claim that all
legal trajectories are bounded or that every legal alternative remains closable under the
frozen ordinary reference replanner. The detour allowance is a measurement condition and enters
neither VTDO Energy, Contribution, nor Novelty.

`finance_v26_bounded_dynamic_interaction_policy:43126b403dd060baade1e5994306982159b02a0451865a9fda2c54da3fda2b0b`.
