The stages answer two prospective engineering questions:

1. Is the canonical public Candidate space complete, non-Oracle, distractor-preserving, and
   presentation-neutral under the 48 frozen Compiler paths?

   dynamic resource closure before any online execution?

The result is not an online Flash result. In particular, it does not show that Flash can select a
correct action_id, recover from a semantic error, close a Program, or produce an independently
valid trajectory.
