## Audited Root Cause

two direct constructors in `ScriptedTransport.send` and two in `_project_public_payload`, but it
did not enumerate all control-flow exits carrying that exception type. The frozen transport also
contains:

```python

if isinstance(value, TypedTransportFailure):
```

outside the declared four-callsite Source Contract. An exact v26.209 base exception with a
registered `instrument_failure` terminal and arbitrary reason could therefore pass v26.215's
class, origin, terminal, Registry, and invocation-record checks even though no declared direct
constructor created it.

The first failed v26.215 Gate is consequently its exact-source Contract Gate. The four direct
controls remain true; they do not compensate for the unbound `raise value` exit.
